About

I work the part of security that happens at 3 a.m.: the alert nobody wrote a runbook for.

At Raya Data Center I lead detection, triage and incident response across multi-tenant environments, using SIEM and EDR platforms such as Elastic and CrowdStrike. I also keep the security tools tuned, apply threat intelligence, and support ISO 27001 and PCI DSS audit cycles, from gap analysis to closed findings.

I started as a SOC trainee and in technical support, and moved up to a senior engineering role. I also teach security as a freelance instructor with Global Brand on the Depi scholarship program. I like mentoring, and I like turning a messy incident into a clear record someone else can learn from.
